## How many reels change
- **32 of 34 reels** (all 20 brand reels, 12 of 14 editorial reels) carry at least one tag that gets renamed or moved.
- **18 of 31 brands** carry at least one such mood.
- No reel, brand or tag is deleted. Duplicates are merged, so a reel tagged "serene, calm" ends up with just "Calm".
- Before the move I save a copy of every reel's and brand's original tags so the change can be undone.
