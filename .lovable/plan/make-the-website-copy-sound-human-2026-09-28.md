# Make the website copy sound human

## What will change
- Rewrite the public Home, Features, Examples, Pricing, About, Help, Blog, Sign-up, and Sign-in copy in a plain, conversational voice.
- Remove vague marketing phrases, repeated slogans, inflated claims, and wording that sounds mechanically polished.
- Keep product facts, prices, plan limits, legal meaning, links, and all bracketed placeholders unchanged.
- Leave the logged-in app and its workflows unchanged.

## Review
- Check every public page after the edits for readability, layout overflow, and errors.
- Confirm the shared plan copy still matches the product rules.
